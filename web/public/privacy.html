Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026

What I’m Reading (whatiamreading.com) is Andreas Duess’s home library, shown as a bookshelf. It is run by Andreas Duess in Toronto, Canada. This page says what the site learns about you and what it does with it. The short version: there are no accounts, and the only thing you can send is a book recommendation.

What the site collects

Visit counts, through Google Analytics. The site uses a Google tag to count visits: which pages are opened, roughly where visitors are (country or city), what kind of device and browser they use, and which site sent them. Google sets cookies to do this and handles the information under its own privacy policy.

If you are in the European Union, the wider European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Google tag is loaded only if you press “Accept” on the cookie notice. If you press “No thanks”, it is never loaded.

Server records. The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like every web host, Cloudflare sees the internet address (IP address) of each request and keeps short-lived records to run the service and stop abuse. To decide whether to show the cookie notice, the site asks Cloudflare which country a visit comes from. That answer is not stored.

The “Recommend a book” form

If you recommend a book, the site keeps what you entered: the title, the author, your note, and your name and email address if you gave them, together with your country and the time. It also keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, which cannot be turned back into the address and is used only to limit how many recommendations one visitor can send in an hour. Your recommendation is stored on Cloudflare and emailed to Andreas.

Your email address is used only to reply to you. Recommendations are kept until Andreas deletes them. Write to andreas@duess.com and yours will be deleted.

To keep out automated spam, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which runs in the background when you open the form. It looks at signals from your browser to tell people from robots and does not set advertising cookies. Cloudflare describes it in its Turnstile privacy addendum.

What is kept in your browser

The site saves your cookie choice in your own browser’s storage, so it does not ask again. It stays on your device, is not a cookie and is not sent anywhere.

Other services your browser talks to

Each of these sees your IP address, as any website you visit does. The site sends them nothing else about you.

What the site does not do

  • It does not sell or share information about visitors.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not build profiles of visitors.

Your choices and rights

You can use the site with cookies blocked; the shelves work the same. You can change your cookie choice with the link above, or clear the site’s data in your browser’s settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. To ask anything about privacy, write to andreas@duess.com. If you are in Canada and are not satisfied with the answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. If you are in Europe, you can contact your local data protection authority.
